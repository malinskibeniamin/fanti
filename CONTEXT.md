# Domain glossary

## Character catalog

The complete set of Chinese glyphs Fanti can identify from its pinned CC-CEDICT and Unihan sources. Catalog membership does not imply that every learning capability is available.

## Curriculum character

A catalog character with both a reading and meaning. Curriculum characters are eligible for Fanti's automatic learning path: frequency-ranked characters first, followed by the remaining CC-CEDICT characters.

## Core curriculum

The first 3,000 frequency-ranked curriculum characters. Core progress remains visible as a practical literacy milestone even though the automatic learning path continues beyond it.

## Complete curriculum

All CC-CEDICT curriculum characters. Its learning path follows every available frequency rank before placing unranked entries in stable Unicode order.

## Reference character

A catalog character that is searchable and may be added to study manually, but is excluded from automatic lessons because required curriculum content, such as a meaning, is missing.

## Learning entry

The unit a learner studies. A CC-CEDICT learning entry is keyed by its Traditional form and may expose a related Simplified form; ambiguous Simplified mappings remain distinct entries. A Unihan-only glyph is its own learning entry.

Progress for Core and Complete curricula counts only their eligible learning entries. Manually learned Reference characters are tracked separately.

A learning entry may have multiple sourced readings, senses, and Simplified forms. Fanti preserves all of them while selecting one preferred everyday reading for compact lists and introductory character lessons. Character-level mastery does not claim mastery of every context-specific reading; those belong to word and sense learning.

## Character capability

One independently available kind of character knowledge, such as reading, meaning, stroke order, component breakdown, or historical forms. A missing capability is disclosed explicitly and does not remove the character from the catalog.

Study exercises adapt to these capabilities. Fanti never fabricates missing content or asks a question that the available character knowledge cannot support.

A capability has one of three states:

- **Available** — Fanti has usable source data.
- **Not applicable** — the capability does not apply, such as a component breakdown for an atomic character.
- **Unavailable** — the capability applies but Fanti's current sources do not provide it.

Reading and meaning capabilities belong to the learning entry. Form-dependent capabilities, including stroke order, components, and historical forms, are evaluated separately for each Traditional or Simplified form.
